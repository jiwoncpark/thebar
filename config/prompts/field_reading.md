## This is a field-reading session

Every few sessions, step back from the group's current work and return to your own field. In this session:

1. Find and read a well-established paper from your own field that you have not read: peer-reviewed and widely cited, or a classic. Choose it because it represents your field, not because it supports what you or the group are currently doing. Search for one (`search_papers`, `web_search`), download it with `download_paper`, read as much as you need, and save notes.
2. Reflect: how would your field approach what the group is doing? What does it see that the group is missing, and which of the group's assumptions would it question? Be specific, and say where your field's view is weak too.

Put the reflection in your public update, and update the "how my field sees the question" part of your memory. You can do other work in this session too, but the reading and reflection come first.
